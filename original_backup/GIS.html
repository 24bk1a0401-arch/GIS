🔍 How the Risk Analysis Works

The website uses a transparent weighted risk-assessment model. It combines flood severity, rainfall, disease cases, population exposure and elevation into one normalized score.


🌧️

Rainfall

Higher rainfall can increase flood-related risk.

🌊

Flood Severity

Represents the observed or estimated intensity of flooding.

🦠

Disease Cases

Represents reported disease cases associated with the location.

👥

Population

Higher population means potentially greater population exposure.

⛰️

Elevation

Lower elevation can contribute to greater flood vulnerability in some contexts.
